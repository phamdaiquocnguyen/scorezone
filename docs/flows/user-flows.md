# User Flows

## 1. Homepage Discovery

The homepage acts as the main entry point to ScoreZone.

### Hero Banner
- Click **"Đọc ngay"** → News Detail

### Match Center
- **Hôm nay** → view today's fixtures and live matches
- **Kết quả** → view completed matches
- Click a match card → Match Detail

### Featured Videos
- Click a video → Video Detail

### Followed Teams
- Click a team → Club Detail
- Click **"Thêm đội"** → follow/select team flow

### Latest News
- Click featured article → News Detail
- Click a news card → News Detail

### Supporting Information
- Click **"Chi tiết"** in Standings → Standings
- Top Scorers → no dedicated detail screen yet
- Featured Match → Match Detail


## 2. Fixture Discovery

Users can discover matches through the **Lịch & Kết quả** page.

### Entry points

- Homepage → **Xem lịch thi đấu**
- Main navigation → **Lịch & Kết quả**

### Browse fixtures

Users can:
- Filter by competition
- Change the selected date
- View live matches
- View upcoming matches
- View today's results

### Match selection

Clicking a match from any of these sections opens **Match Detail**.

### Match Detail

The Match Detail page provides:
- Match overview
- AI Match Insight
- Match video
- Important match information
- Line-up view

The **Đội hình** tab allows users to explore starting line-ups, substitutes, and unavailable players.


## 3. Discover from League

Users can explore football information from a competition level down to individual players.

### League discovery

- Open **Giải đấu**
- Select a featured competition or league card
- Open **Competition Detail**

### Competition

From Competition Detail, users can:
- View standings
- Read the latest competition news
- Open recent match results
- Browse clubs through the **Câu lạc bộ** tab

### Club

- Select a club
- Open **Club Detail**
- View club overview, latest news, team information, and standings position
- Switch to **Đội hình** to browse players

### Player

- Select a player card
- Open **Player Detail**
- Browse player information and related teammates

> The flow moves from broad competition-level information to club-level and finally player-level details.


## 4. News Discovery

The News section allows users to discover football articles and video highlights.

### News

Users can:

- Browse all news
- Open the featured article
- Open individual news cards
- Filter content
- Search for news
- Sort content

Clicking an article opens **News Detail**.

### Video Highlights

Users can switch to **Video nổi bật** to browse football highlights.

Clicking a video opens **Video Detail**.

### News Detail

News Detail includes:

- Article content
- AI Summary
- Related videos
- Related news
- Comments

### Video Detail

Video Detail includes:

- Video player
- Video information
- Related highlights

> News and video content are connected through related-content modules, allowing users to move between articles and highlights.


## 5. Standings Discovery

The Standings page allows users to browse competition rankings and top scorers.

### Browse standings

Users can:

- Switch between competitions such as Premier League and UEFA Champions League.
- Select a season.
- View team ranking, points, form, and upcoming opponents.
- View the top scorers for the selected competition.

### Team exploration

Selecting a team from the standings can lead to its **Club Detail** page.

> Competition and season changes update the displayed standings and related top-scorer information.
